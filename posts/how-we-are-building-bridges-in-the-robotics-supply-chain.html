How We Are Building Bridges in the Robotics Supply Chain

December 27, 2025

Reading the news today — and I hope you did your Christmas drone shopping early — I'm reflecting on what we're building at Remake.ai:

  • infrastructure to bring innovative, affordable robots to US and European customers.

The robotics industry is entering a new phase of supply chain complexity:

  • Connected vehicle software rules take effect in 2027.
  • Drone import regulations have tightened.
  • A Section 232 investigation into robotics and industrial machinery is underway.

The trend is clear - manufacturers who want to continue to sell globally need infrastructure that works globally.

US and European customers want access to innovative, affordable robots — vacuums today, quadrupeds and humanoids tomorrow.

Manufacturers want to serve those customers. The challenge is building infrastructure that satisfies everyone: customers, manufacturers, and regulators.

That's what we're building at Remake.ai.

Our platform gives robot manufacturers a path to global markets:

  • US/EU-based IoT infrastructure, built for compliance
  • US-based ownership
  • Drop-in compatibility
  • Turnkey solution

Great consumer robots for everyone — built on infrastructure everyone can trust.
